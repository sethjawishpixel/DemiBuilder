🔥 Cap Breaker Results

Rules (NBA 2K27): each attribute can take at most 5 Cap Breakers, and applying one is permanent. An attribute can't go past the max potential allowed by your build's height, weight and wingspan, so check your cap in the Builder. Cap Breakers from Build Specialization must go into the category you specialized in (or Free Throw / a Physicals attribute, depending on the source). Click a chip under any attribute to set how many Cap Breakers it gets; each chip shows the rating after that breaker.
