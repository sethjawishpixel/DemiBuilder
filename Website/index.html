🔥 Cap Breaker Results

Rules (NBA 2K27): each attribute can take at most 5 Cap Breakers, and applying one is permanent. Every attribute has a max potential set by your position, height, weight and wingspan, and Cap Breakers can only push it up to that attribute's Cap Breaker limit. Attributes with low caps get bigger boosts per breaker, and an attribute already near 99 gets little or nothing. DemiBuilder's caps and limits are its own estimates, not official 2K numbers, so check the in-game MyPLAYER Builder before you spend anything. Cap Breakers from Build Specialization must go into the category you specialized in (or Free Throw / a Physicals attribute, depending on the source). Click a chip under any attribute to set how many Cap Breakers it gets; each chip shows the rating after that breaker.
